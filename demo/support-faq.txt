# PipelineKit — Support FAQ (demo company)
# B2B SaaS, ~20 people. Seed this into knowledge for realistic widget demos.

## Password reset
Go to Settings → Security → Reset Password. We email a reset link within 5 minutes.
If you use SSO (Google or Okta), reset the password with your identity provider — not inside PipelineKit.
Still locked out after two tries? Escalate to a human — do not share passwords in chat.

## Billing and invoices
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal. Invoices go out on the 1st of each month to the billing email on the workspace.
Failed card charges retry for 3 days, then the workspace moves to read-only until payment succeeds.
Plan changes (upgrade/downgrade) take effect on the next billing cycle unless you ask support to prorate.

## Refund policy
Full refund within 14 days of first paid invoice if you have not exceeded fair-use API limits.
After 14 days, refunds are case-by-case (billing errors, prolonged outages). Chargebacks should go through support first.

## API rate limits
Free: 100 requests/day. Pro: 10,000 requests/day. Enterprise: custom.
429 responses mean you hit the cap — wait for the reset window or upgrade. Burst spikes during imports are a common cause.

## Support hours and escalation
Live agents: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM Eastern.
AI chat is 24/7 for policy and how-to questions grounded in this FAQ.
Escalate to a ticket when: SSO/MFA fails, you suspect a bug, billing dispute, or the AI answer is wrong.
We aim to reply to escalations within 1 business day (4 hours for SSO lockouts).

## Integrations
We support webhooks and a REST API. Slack Connect is available on Pro+.
If a sync job fails twice, open a ticket and include the workspace ID and approximate time of failure.

## What the AI cannot do
We cannot process refunds, change roles, or disable SSO from chat.
We cannot access another customer's data. For account takeover concerns, escalate immediately.
