# Design-partner outreach (real SMB shapes)

Use **real companies you can contact**. Do not invent testimonials. Personas below are templates for who to chase and what to pitch.

Grounding: [`docs/REAL_WORLD_USE.md`](../../docs/REAL_WORLD_USE.md)

## Who to contact (by persona)

| Persona | Look for | Pitch angle | Ask |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----|
| **GlowTheory-type** | Shopify D2C, 8–20 people, returns/WISMO pain | Widget for returns + shipping FAQ; escalate disputes | 30-day pilot; weekly metrics |
| **PipelineKit-type** | B2B SaaS, Intercom/email, founder still on support | Password/billing deflection + ticket with transcript | Upload docs; 20 widget sessions |
| **Parkview-type** | Clinic / local service with a website | Hours + insurance FAQ (booking stays in their PMS) | Embed on contact page |
| **Hall’s-type** | HVAC / trades with “request service” form | Triage emergency vs callback FAQ | After-hours web deflection |
| **SummitPrep-type** | Tutoring / agencies that hire often | Parent FAQ **and** tutor resume shortlist | Dual-lane pilot |

## 5-line pitch (copy/adapt)

> Hi {{name}} — I built OpsConcierge for teams like yours that still answer the same {{returns / password / hours}} questions every week.
> It answers from **your** FAQ on the website widget, escalates to a ticket with the full chat when a human is needed, and logs auditable support runs so you can fix the docs.
> Same workspace can shortlist resumes when you’re hiring (you still post on Indeed — we don’t replace that).
> 15-min live demo: {{demo URL}}. No long contract for pilots.
> Open to a quick call this week?

---

## What “good” pilot success looks like

- ≥40% of widget questions resolved without a ticket (routine FAQs only)
- Every escalation includes transcript (no “repeat yourself to the human”)
- Owner spent ≤30 min/week updating FAQ from the execution log
- Hiring (if used): founder reviewed a ranked top-10 instead of every PDF

---

## Do not

- Claim 90% autonomous support
- Auto-reject candidates in the pitch
- Fabricate logos or quotes
