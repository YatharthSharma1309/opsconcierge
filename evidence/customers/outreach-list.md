Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? I'd love to embed a test widget on _[their site / staging]_ and get your feedback. No cost for the pilot.

_[Your name]_

---

## Call script (15 min)

1. Their current support/hiring pain (5 min)
2. Live demo — widget → ticket → execution log (5 min)
3. Pilot terms: free Pro for 30 days, weekly 10-min check-in (3 min)
4. Ask permission for anonymized quote if successful (2 min)

---

## Success criteria for pilot

- [ ] Widget embedded (staging or production)
- [ ] ≥10 real customer conversations
- [ ] ≥1 escalation → ticket
- [ ] Feedback notes captured
- [ ] Testimonial request (see `testimonials.md`) — only if they agree
