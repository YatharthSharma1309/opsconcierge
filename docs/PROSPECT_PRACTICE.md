## Fictional rehearsal set

These six companies are invented. They are for practicing the three offers. They have no websites, no inboxes, and no people to contact.

| Name | Invented shape | Question to practice |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| Northline Metrics | 12-person analytics SaaS. Founder still answers “why is this number blank?” from the docs. | Would you pay monthly for support only? |
| Clearledger | 7-person invoicing tool for agencies. Repeat questions are tax settings and failed payments. Hires one support person a year. | Support only, hiring only, or both? |
| Dockline | 14-person API product. Docs exist. The founder handles billing and rate-limit threads at night. | Is the docs-grounded widget enough, or do they need refund actions? |
| Rosterly | 18-person scheduling SaaS. Help center is a Notion export. Two founders share the inbox. | What do they pay now, and what broke in the last chatbot? |
| Fieldnote | 9-person sales-notes tool. A hiring sprint happens about twice a year. Support is weekly. | Does hiring change the purchase, or only support? |
| Deskform | 11-person form backend. Public docs. No support hire. | Would a 30-day pilot on their real docs be worth a conversation? |

Ask each fictional founder the same three offers, and write a pretend answer only in a practice note. A real answer replaces this table only after a real conversation.
